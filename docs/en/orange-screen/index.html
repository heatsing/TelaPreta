A Solid Color for Display Testing

The preview starts at #FFA500. Fullscreen mode fills the entire test area with this color.

What to Look For

Orange Screen

Compare the center and edges for tint changes or use the orange as a solid PNG backdrop.

How to Check Your Display

  1. Open the preview on the display you want to test and set it to your usual brightness.
  2. Scan the center, corners, and edges. Switch colors to compare the same spot.

Use the button in the lower-right corner of the preview to see the color in fullscreen. Click or tap the screen, or press Esc to exit.

What to look for on each color

  • BlackLook for tiny dots that stay lit. On an LCD, check the edges as well.
  • WhiteLook for dark dots, patches of tint, or dimmer areas.
  • Red, green, and blueSee whether a spot changes as you switch between the primary colors.
  • GrayCompare the brightness and tint from one area of the display to another.
